Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

SharedClassrooms ("we", "us") connects tuition centers with spare classroom time to tutors looking for group class space in Singapore. This page explains what information we collect, why, and how it's handled.

What we collect

From tuition centers listing a space: center name, contact person, phone, email, address, and details about the classroom(s) being listed (capacity, pricing, amenities, availability, and an optional photo).

From tutors making a booking inquiry: name, phone or email, and the details of the booking request (date, time, duration, and any notes you provide).

From Google Sign-In (Manage Listing): only the email address associated with your Google account, used to match you to your center's listing. We never see or store your Google password, and we don't request access to your Gmail, Calendar, Drive, or any other Google data — only your basic sign-in email.

How we use it

  • To display verified classroom listings to tutors browsing the marketplace.
  • To let a center owner manage and update their own listing.
  • To pass booking inquiries from tutors to the relevant center so they can arrange the booking directly.
  • To physically verify a listing before it goes live, and to re-verify it if details change.

How it's stored

Listing and inquiry data is stored in a Supabase-hosted database. Google Sign-In is verified using Google's own sign-in service; we do not run any tracking scripts, advertising pixels, or third-party analytics on this site.

Who we share it with

We don't sell or rent your information to third parties. A tutor's booking inquiry is shared with the relevant tuition center so they can respond to it — that's the only routine sharing that happens. We may disclose information if required to by law.

Your choices

You can ask us to correct or delete information you've submitted at any time by contacting us (see below). Center owners can update most of their own listing details directly through the Manage Listing page.

Singapore Personal Data Protection Act (PDPA)

As a Singapore-based service, we aim to handle personal data consistently with the PDPA's principles of consent, purpose limitation, and reasonable protection. This policy is a plain-language description of our current practices and has not been reviewed by a lawyer — if you have specific compliance questions, please contact us.
